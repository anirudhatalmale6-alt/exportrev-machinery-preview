# ExportRev Machinery — working preview

Live: https://anirudhatalmale6-alt.github.io/exportrev-machinery-preview/

Construction and industrial equipment: trucks, excavators, loaders, cranes, bulldozers,
dumpers, concrete equipment, material handling, road construction, drilling, mining,
industrial equipment and spare parts. Masonry grid, filters that filter, a detail sheet
and a feedback section. No framework, no build step, no tracking.

Styled to match **exportrev.com**: its hero green (#22b61e), its button green (#61ce70), its
light body (#f4f4f4), its Roboto typeface and its diagonal hero cut. Those values were read
off the live site, not guessed from a screenshot, and the check suite asserts the palette
cannot quietly drift back to the aircraft preview's dark red.

## What is on the page, and what is deliberately not

The inventory is **demonstration data**, generated by `gen_data.py` from a fixed seed.
Nothing is copied from Machineryline or from any other marketplace: no listing, no
description, no dealer name, no telephone number, no photograph of theirs.

Makes and models are real product names, and each model is bound to the manufacturer
that actually builds it. Pairing a random make with a random model inside a category
produces things like "DOOSAN R 926" — a Liebherr model under a Doosan badge — which a
plant buyer spots instantly. The check suite asserts three of those pairings can never
reappear.

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under licences that permit reuse, are served
from this site rather than hotlinked, and print their author and licence on the card.
Machines with no usable photo keep the drawn silhouette — one outline per family, so a
crane is never drawn as a dumper.

## Files

    index.html          the page
    assets/style.css    one stylesheet, shared with the aircraft preview
    assets/app.js       filtering, masonry, silhouettes, detail sheet, feedback form
    assets/data.js      generated demonstration inventory
    assets/photos.js    photo credits, one record per machine
    assets/photos/      the photographs themselves
    gen_data.py         the inventory generator
    fetch_photos.py     sources and vets the photographs
    src/checks.py       the automatic check suite

## Checks

    python3 src/checks.py                      # against the published page
    python3 src/checks.py http://127.0.0.1:8000/

82 checks, run against the published URL. They are written to be able to fail — and
they have: the suite caught a real defect where the price slider's `step` did not land
on the top price, so dragging the ceiling fully right still hid the most expensive
machine on the site. The ceiling is now rounded up onto the step grid.
